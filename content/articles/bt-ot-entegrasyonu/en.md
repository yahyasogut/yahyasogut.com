Integrating production systems with the ERP means joining two worlds: automation that runs in milliseconds and an ERP that runs on business processes.

## 1. Write a shared data dictionary first
What does "batch" mean on the floor, and in the ERP? If two teams read the same word differently, the integration will be wrong too.

## 2. Separate the OT network from IT
Integration must not mean exposing the production network. Layered networks and one-way data flows are essential.

## 3. Move the data that drives decisions, not everything
Not every sensor reading belongs in the ERP. Confirmations, consumption and quality results are enough.

## 4. Design the failure path up front
What happens to data when the connection drops? Queues, retries and alerts belong in day one.
