## Problem
User accounts were managed separately across systems, and security incidents were only noticed after the fact.

## Approach
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) for every user
- Central identity management and role-based access
- Centralised log collection with alerting rules
- Phishing awareness training for employees

## Outcome
Unauthorised access attempts became visible instantly, and audit preparation time dropped sharply.
